# Verifica Pix

## Register

Brand documentation with product demonstrations. The brandbook must communicate the
identity and also show how the identity behaves inside a real SaaS product.

## Product Purpose

Verifica Pix helps merchants inspect Pix receipts before releasing a product. It
organizes evidence, highlights inconsistencies and supports a more confident
operational decision. It does not replace bank confirmation and does not promise
fraud prevention with absolute certainty.

## Primary Users

- Business owners who are not always present at checkout.
- Cashiers and attendants who need a clear decision flow.
- Operations teams responsible for defining verification procedures.
- Designers, developers, partners and copywriters implementing the brand.

## Core Problem

The "Brecha do Caixa Cego": the moment between receiving a receipt and releasing a
product, when an employee has incomplete context and the owner has little visibility.

## Positioning

Verifica Pix is an operational confidence layer between the receipt and the release
of the product.

## Central Idea

Antes de liberar o produto, verifique o Pix.

## Promise

Você não fica sozinho na decisão.

## Personality

- Protective, without creating fear.
- Precise, without sounding bureaucratic.
- Clear, especially when the evidence is inconclusive.
- Human, without becoming informal or playful.
- Honest about technical and legal limits.

## Voice

Direct, calm, empathetic and active. Prefer short sentences and explicit next steps.
Never claim that a transaction is definitively safe, paid or fraudulent based only
on receipt analysis.

## Product Status Language

- Baixo risco aparente: no relevant inconsistency was found in the available evidence.
- Atenção: some evidence needs manual review.
- Alto risco aparente: relevant inconsistencies were detected; do not release without
  additional confirmation.

Every status must include the reminder that final settlement should be confirmed in
the receiving bank account.

## Strategic Principles

1. Show the product before describing the system.
2. Turn uncertainty into a clear next action.
3. Use green for progress and confirmation, not as proof of settlement.
4. Use navy for structure, authority and long-form reading.
5. Make every implementation rule easy to find and copy.
6. Design for a rushed cashier and a cautious owner at the same time.

## References

- ClickUp home: product-led narrative, strong hierarchy and interface demonstrations.
- ClickUp Tasks: one problem per section, concrete outcomes and connected feature story.
- AI Website Cloner Template: inspect, extract tokens, specify components, then build.

These are structural references only. The Verifica Pix identity must remain original.

## Anti-References

- Cold bank portals and government-style documentation.
- Generic SaaS card grids with decorative icons.
- Neon fintech, glassmorphism and artificial security imagery.
- Fear-based fraud copy or absolute guarantees.
- Childish illustrations, emojis and celebratory motion for risk states.

## Accessibility And Quality

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keyboard navigation, visible focus, 44px touch targets,
reduced-motion support, semantic landmarks and status communication that never
depends on color alone are required.
