# Reference Page Topology

Reference inspected: `https://meuassessor.com/`

The implementation must reuse the conversion architecture, not the source brand,
copy, metrics or media assets.

## Observed Structure

1. Sticky dark navigation with section links and one primary CTA.
2. Dark hero with oversized headline, short explanation and a product conversation.
3. Continuous proof strip.
4. Three light feature sections with alternating copy and product UI.
5. Dark interruption section with a compact promise and CTA.
6. Long dark product capability sequence.
7. Light collaboration section.
8. Large statement plus a grid of possible actions.
9. Saturated color section with multiple product demonstrations.
10. Light document/productivity section.
11. Dark results and social proof section.
12. Light pricing/offer section.
13. Dark FAQ.
14. Institutional closing.
15. Dark footer.

## Interaction Model

- Header remains visible while scrolling.
- Navigation links use anchor scrolling.
- Content appears with restrained entrance transitions.
- Product demonstrations are static compositions with small state cues.
- FAQ uses expandable rows.
- Responsive behavior changes from alternating two-column sections to one column.

## Verifica Pix Adaptation

- Hero: receipt analysis and operational decision.
- Proof strip: capabilities, not fabricated customer metrics.
- Feature sequence: receive, compare, decide.
- Dark capability block: risk engine and evidence trail.
- Collaboration: owner, cashier and operations.
- Action grid: common verification tasks.
- Offer: access to project and brandbook, without invented pricing.
- FAQ: technical and legal limits of receipt analysis.
